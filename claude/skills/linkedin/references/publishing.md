# Publishing: Cadence, Windows, and Metrics

## Cadence

- **4 posts/week** is the sweet spot (range 3-5).
- Never more than 1 per day; 8+ per week drops ER to ~1.79%.
- 200 posts ≈ 1 year at 4 per week.
- Round-robin interleaving (the generator does it) avoids saturating one niche,
  but dedicating weekly blocks to your 2-3 strong topics is better.

## Recommended windows

- Main: **Wednesday 15:00-16:00** and **Friday 15:00-16:00**.
- Alternative: Tuesday to Thursday, 7:00-9:00 and 12:00-14:00.
- Each `.md` carries a rotated suggested window; validate with your own stats
  after 4-8 weeks and adjust.

## Golden hour (first 30-90 min)

1. Before publishing, spend 10-15 min commenting on accounts in your niche.
2. Publish inside the chosen window.
3. Reply to **every** comment within 60-90 min; each reply reactivates scoring.
4. Do not edit the post afterwards: it resets the initial distribution.

## Suggested topic blocks

The pack has 12 topics. For the Interest Graph, publish in blocks:

- Month 1: TypeScript + web development + xscriptor (your strong topics).
- Month 2: accessibility + usability + programming.
- Month 3: JavaScript + VS Code + extensions.
- Month 4: ecosystem + distros + career.
- Repeat the cycle with the remaining hooks.

Keep 70-80% of content in 2-3 areas of expertise; rotate the rest.

## Metrics to track (weekly)

- **Dwell time** and reach per post (what the algorithm looks at first).
- **Saves**: the strongest signal; no saves means the post teaches little.
- **Comments** (and replies inside the thread).
- **Followers gained per post**, not just impressions.
- Profile click rate.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] The hook fits in 2 lines and is not misleading.
- [ ] The post exceeds 800 characters and has 4 concrete ideas.
- [ ] Exactly 3 niche hashtags.
- [ ] No external links in the body.
- [ ] 1080x1350 image with the right number and ALT pasted into LinkedIn.
- [ ] Valid time window and 30-60 free minutes afterwards for comments.
