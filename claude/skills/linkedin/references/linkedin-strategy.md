# LinkedIn Strategy (2025-2026)

Operational summary of what rewards and sinks posts, with the numbers that
matter when planning.

## What the algorithm rewards

- **Dwell time** (reading time) is the core metric. LinkedIn penalizes predicted
  "short dwell": if the reader leaves after 3 seconds, the post sinks.
  - Document carousel: ~55 s of reading time vs. ~15 s for a text post.
  - 61 s or more: 15.6% engagement; under 3 s: 1.2%.
- **Interaction hierarchy**: a save ≈ 5x a like and ~2x a comment; a comment ≈ 2x
  a like; comment threads up to 2.4x reach; a plain repost only 0.29x.
- **The first 2-3 lines** (~140 characters on mobile, ~210 on desktop) decide
  whether "see more" is tapped. Misleading hooks that cause quick exits penalize.
- **Consistent interest (Interest/Knowledge Graph)**: 70-80% of content should
  fall within 2-3 areas of expertise. Going off-niche reduces reach.
- **Early comments**: replying to every comment within the first 30-90 min
  reactivates scoring (+30% typical engagement).

## What gets penalized

- **External links in the body**: ~-60% reach. "Link in first comment" is also
  penalized. Publish natively; keep the link in the profile or add it later by
  editing the post.
- **Bait**: "comment YES", "like if…" is suppressed by NLP. Engagement pods are
  prohibited (detection ~97%).
- **Generic AI content without a personal perspective**: ~-30% reach and
  -55% engagement.
- Posting more than once a day; more than 8 posts/week drops ER to 1.79%.
- Too many hashtags (6+ sinks; 3-5 slightly reduces; **the optimum is 3 or fewer**).

## Format that performs

- 800-1,000+ characters per post (1,000+ = 1.18x reach; under 300 = 0.88x).
- 16-20 sentences, 1-3 line paragraphs, easy reading (~4th grade level).
- Bullets, arrows (→), unicode bold, 0-2 emojis, no markdown.
- An open, specific closing question to drive comments.

## Image

- **1080x1350 px (4:5)** performs best for a single image (2.83% ER vs. 2.54%
  square and 2.14% horizontal). LinkedIn crops above 1350 px in height.
- Text inside the image: no formal penalty, but screen readers cannot read it →
  **always ALT** and keep the key message in the copy too.
- PDF carousel (6-10 slides, 1080x1350): 1.39x reach and 1.30x engagement; worth
  it above ~20K followers rather than a single image.

## Profile and consistency

- A profile aligned with the topic (headline, featured) multiplies each post's reach.
- Avoid editing the post after publishing (it resets initial distribution).
- 3-5 posts/week: 4-5 per week yields ~2.60% ER and ~870 impressions.
